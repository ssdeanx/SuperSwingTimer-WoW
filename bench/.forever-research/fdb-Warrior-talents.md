# Warrior talent calculator and leveling order

[Rotations & Guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior) · [PvE guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/pve) · [PvP guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/pvp) · [Talent builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/builds) · [Abilities](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/abilities) · [Talent Calculator](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/talents) · [Macros](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/macros) · [Changes vs Classic](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/changes)

Other classes: [Paladin](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/talents), [Hunter](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/talents), [Rogue](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/talents), [Priest](https://foreverdb.net/class/Priest/talents), [Shaman](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/talents), [Mage](https://foreverdb.net/class/Mage/talents), [Warlock](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warlock/talents), [Druid](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/talents)

Warriors are rage-fuelled melee fighters that excel as tanks or two-handed/dual-wield damage dealers. Forever makes Tactical Mastery baseline so stance dancing keeps rage, reworks Slam into a 15 sec cooldown strike (Improved Slam, an Arms tier 6 talent, shortens the cooldown and stops Slam delaying your swing), and adds Spearing Strike, an Arms talent for dismounting and hitting giants.

Plan your Warrior talents for WoW Forever: click talents into the three trees and the calculator records the order you take them in, one talent per level, so you know what to pick at every level-up. Drag a level to move it, or click it to change its pick; every level is checked against the tree's tier and prerequisite rules. Share the build as a short link, load a recommended build in one click, and send the plan to the ForeverDB addon, which tells you where each new point goes in game.

## Talent points in WoW Forever

From the Forever client (TraitCurrencySource): one talent point per level from 10 to 60, 51 in all. The Legacy perk Talented gives the same points up to 5 levels sooner (one more rank each at levels 9, 8, 7, 6 and 5). In each section a row needs 5 more points spent in the rows above it.

## Warrior talents compared with Classic

Of the 53 talents in Forever's Warrior tree, 11 are new, 24 changed from Classic Era and 18 are the same. Classic talents Forever doesn't have: Sword Specialization (Arms tier 5), Polearm Specialization (Arms tier 6), Improved Battle Shout (Fury tier 3), Toughness (Protection tier 2), Improved Taunt (Protection tier 4).

## Arms talents

-   Tier 1: [Improved Heroic Strike](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12282) (3 ranks, level 10+): Reduces the cost of your Heroic Strike ability by 3 Rage.
-   Tier 1: [Deflection](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16462) (5 ranks, level 10+): Increases your Parry chance by 5%.
-   Tier 1: [Improved Rend](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12286) (changed from Classic) (3 ranks, level 10+): Increases the Bleed damage done by your Rend ability by 35%. Classic: Increases the bleed damage done by your Rend ability by 35%.
-   Tier 2: [Improved Charge](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12285) (2 ranks, level 15+): Increases the Rage generated by your Charge ability by 6.
-   Tier 2: [Improved Tactical Mastery](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12295) (changed from Classic) (3 ranks, level 15+): Tactical Mastery lets you retain up to an additional 15 Rage when you change stances. Classic: You retain up to 25 of your rage points when you change stances.
-   Tier 2: [Improved Overpower](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12290) (2 ranks, level 15+): Increases the critical strike chance of your Overpower ability by 50%.
-   Tier 3: [Anger Management](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12296) (changed from Classic) (1 rank, level 20+): Generates 1 Rage every 3 sec while in combat, and reduces Rage loss while out of combat by 30%. Classic: Increases the time required for your rage to decay while out of combat by 30%.
-   Tier 3: [Deep Wounds](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12834) (3 ranks, level 20+): Your critical strikes cause your opponent to Bleed, dealing 60% of your melee weapon's average damage over 12 sec.
-   Tier 4: [Spearing Strike](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310222) (new in Forever) (1 rank, level 25+): A brutal attack that deals 75% weapon damage. Deals an additional 75% weapon damage against Giants, Dragonkin, and mounted targets. Mounted targets are dismounted.
-   Tier 4: [Two-Handed Weapon Specialization](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12163) (changed from Classic) (3 ranks, level 25+): Increases the damage you deal with two-handed melee weapons by 6%. Classic: Increases the damage you deal with two-handed melee weapons by 5%.
-   Tier 4: [Impale](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16493) (2 ranks, level 25+): Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your abilities by 20%.
-   Tier 5: [Bloodthrill](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1289682) (new in Forever) (2 ranks, level 30+): Grants your Main Hand melee attacks against enemies afflicted by your Rend a 20% chance to allow an activation of Overpower.
-   Tier 5: [Sweeping Strikes](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12292) (1 rank, level 30+): Your next 5 melee attacks strike an additional nearby opponent.
-   Tier 5: [Weaponmaster](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1290261) (new in Forever) (5 ranks, level 30+): Gives your melee weapon attacks a benefit depending on the weapon. Axe/Polearm: Increases your critical strike chance by 5%. Mace/Staff: Your attacks ignore 15% of your target's armor. Sword: Your successful melee attacks have a 5% chance to trigger an extra attack on the target.
-   Tier 6: [Improved Slam](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12862) (changed from Classic) (2 ranks, level 35+): Reduces the cooldown of your Slam ability by 5.0 sec, the cast time and global cooldown by 0.50 sec, and Slam no longer interrupts or delays your melee swing. Classic: Decreases the casting time of your Slam ability by 0.5 sec.
-   Tier 6: [Improved Hamstring](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12289) (3 ranks, level 35+): Gives your Hamstring ability a 15% chance to immobilize the target for 5 sec.
-   Tier 7: [Mortal Strike](https://foreverdb.net/spell/12294) (1 rank, level 40+): A vicious strike that deals weapon damage plus 85 and wounds the target, reducing the effectiveness of any healing by 50% for 10 sec.
