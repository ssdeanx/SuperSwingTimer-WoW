Druids are shapeshifting hybrids that can heal, tank in bear form, fight in cat form or cast as Moonkin. Forever adds a real bear toolkit, and the new talent tree adds Lacerate, Feral Charge and Primal Bite for Feral and Wild Growth for Restoration, plus the out-of-combat Revive. Insect Swarm is now a Balance talent, and Omen of Clarity a trainer ability at 20.

Plan your Druid talents for WoW Forever: click talents into the three trees and the calculator records the order you take them in, one talent per level, so you know what to pick at every level-up. Drag a level to move it, or click it to change its pick; every level is checked against the tree's tier and prerequisite rules. Share the build as a short link, load a recommended build in one click, and send the plan to the ForeverDB addon, which tells you where each new point goes in game.

## Talent points in WoW Forever

From the Forever client (TraitCurrencySource): one talent point per level from 10 to 60, 51 in all. The Legacy perk Talented gives the same points up to 5 levels sooner (one more rank each at levels 9, 8, 7, 6 and 5). In each section a row needs 5 more points spent in the rows above it.

## Druid talents compared with Classic

Of the 52 talents in Forever's Druid tree, 14 are new, 23 changed from Classic Era and 15 are the same. Classic talents Forever doesn't have: Improved Thorns (Balance tier 3), Feral Aggression (Feral Combat tier 1), Faerie Fire (Feral) (Feral Combat tier 5), Improved Mark of the Wild (Restoration tier 1), Improved Enrage (Restoration tier 2), Nature's Grasp (Balance tier 1), Omen of Clarity (Balance tier 3).

Seen in WoW Forever: 4 players (4 characters). How it works: signed-in players upload their Companion addon file (its Your talent builds setting records each character’s talents). Each character counts once per level range, at most 2 of one player’s, and no names are shown. A spec appears once 5 characters from 3 different players at that level range have uploaded, and a build once 2 players run it.

## Balance talents

-   Tier 1: [Improved Wrath](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16814) (changed from Classic) (5 ranks, level 10+): Reduces the cast time of your Wrath spell by 0.5 sec and its Mana cost by 50%. Classic: Reduces the cast time of your Wrath spell by 0.5 sec.
-   Tier 1: [Genesis](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1223081) (new in Forever) (5 ranks, level 10+): Increases the periodic damage and healing done by your spells and abilities by 10%.
-   Tier 2: [Moonglow](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16845) (changed from Classic) (3 ranks, level 15+): Reduces the Mana cost of your damaging spells by 25%. Classic: Reduces the Mana cost of your Moonfire, Starfire, Wrath, Healing Touch, Regrowth and Rejuvenation spells by 9%.
-   Tier 2: [Improved Moonfire](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16821) (changed from Classic) (2 ranks, level 15+): Increases the damage and critical strike chance of your Moonfire spell by 10%. Classic: Increases the damage and critical strike chance of your Moonfire spell by 10%.
-   Tier 2: [Nature's Majesty](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1223082) (new in Forever) (2 ranks, level 15+): Increases your critical strike chance with spells and melee attacks by 4%.
-   Tier 2: [Nature's Reach](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16819) (changed from Classic) (2 ranks, level 15+): Increases the range of your offensive Balance spells by 20% and improves your chance to hit by 4%. Classic: Increases the range of your Wrath, Entangling Roots, Faerie Fire, Moonfire, Starfire, and Hurricane spells by 20%.
-   Tier 3: [Improved Entangling Roots](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16918) (changed from Classic) (3 ranks, level 20+): Increases the damage done by your Entangling Roots spell by 75%, and its victims can take up to 75% more damage without interrupting the effect. Classic: Gives you a 100% chance to avoid interruption caused by damage while casting Entangling Roots.
-   Tier 3: [Nature's Splendor](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1223083) (new in Forever) (1 rank, level 20+): Increases the duration of your Moonfire and Rejuvenation spells by 3 sec, your Regrowth spell by 6 sec, and your Insect Swarm spell by 2 sec.
-   Tier 4: [Insect Swarm](https://foreverdb.net/spell/5570) (changed from Classic) (1 rank, level 25+): The enemy target is swarmed by insects, decreasing their chance to hit with attacks by 2% and causing 48 Nature damage over 12 sec. Classic: The enemy target is swarmed by insects, decreasing their chance to hit by 2% and causing 66 Nature damage over 12 sec.
-   Tier 4: [Vengeance](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16909) (5 ranks, level 25+): Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Arcane and Nature spells by 100%.
-   Tier 4: [Improved Starfire](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16850) (5 ranks, level 25+): Reduces the cast time of Starfire by 0.5 sec and Starfire has a 15% chance to stun its target for 3 sec.
-   Tier 5: [Overgrowth](https://foreverdb.net/spell/17245) (changed from Classic) (2 ranks, level 30+): Increases the maximum number of targets you may have affected by Entangling Roots by 2. Classic: Increases the chance for your Nature's Grasp to entangle an enemy by 65%.
-   Tier 5: [Nature's Grace](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16880) (changed from Classic) (1 rank, level 30+): All non-periodic spell criticals grace you with a blessing of nature, increasing your spellcasting speed and reducing your global cooldown by 10% for 3 sec. Classic: All spell criticals grace you with a blessing of nature, reducing the casting time of your next spell by 0.5 sec.
