# Shaman Abilities

[Rotations & Guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman) · [PvE guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/pve) · [PvP guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/pvp) · [Talent builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/builds) · [Abilities](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/abilities) · [Talent Calculator](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/talents) · [Macros](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/macros) · [Changes vs Classic](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/changes)

Other classes: [Warrior](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/abilities), [Paladin](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/abilities), [Hunter](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/abilities), [Rogue](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/abilities), [Priest](https://foreverdb.net/class/Priest/abilities), [Mage](https://foreverdb.net/class/Mage/abilities), [Warlock](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warlock/abilities), [Druid](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/abilities)

Shamans are totem-based hybrids that heal, cast elemental spells or fight in melee. Forever adds Lightning Mastery as the Elemental capstone talent and Riptide as the Restoration capstone talent, Mental Dexterity and Rage of the Farseer for Enhancement, and quality-of-life totem tools: Call of the Elements, Call of the Ancestors and Call of the Spirits drop up to four totems in one 3 sec cast, and Totemic Recall returns them for mana.

## Elemental (Caster DPS)

Elemental opens with Flame Shock, fills with Lightning Bolt and uses Chain Lightning on cooldown for cleave. Elemental Alacrity (tier 2) makes Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning faster and leads to Lightning Overload, Call of Thunder (tier 5) gives both 5% more crit chance, Elemental Fury (tier 6) doubles spell crit bonus, and Lightning Mastery (tier 7, after Call of Thunder) takes 0.5 sec off Lightning Bolt and removes Chain Lightning's cooldown for 15 sec. Call of Flame makes Flame Shock and your Fire totems hit harder.

### Enhancement (Melee DPS)

Enhancement is a melee shaman with Windfury Weapon, Flurry and Stormstrike, backed by powerful group totems. Forever builds the tree around Intellect: Mental Dexterity turns Intellect into attack power, Shamanistic Focus takes 45% off the Mana cost of shocks and Lightning Shield, Stormstrike moves up to tier 4, and Maelstrom Weapon and Rage of the Farseer sit at the bottom. Call of the Elements drops up to four preset totems, such as Windfury, Strength of Earth, Mana Spring and a fire totem, in one 3 sec cast.

### Restoration (Healer)

Restoration Shaman is a group healer with Chain Heal and totems. Forever adds Riptide as the tier 7 Restoration talent: a heal over time that makes Chain Heal cast directly on that target 25% stronger. The loop is Riptide the tank, Chain Heal off them, and fill with Healing Wave or Lesser Healing Wave, while Mindfulness and Water Shield keep your mana flowing.

Best in slot gear at any level: [Elemental](https://foreverdb.net/bis/shaman/elemental), [Enhancement](https://foreverdb.net/bis/shaman/enhancement), [Restoration](https://foreverdb.net/bis/shaman/restoration)
