# Warrior

Warriors train constantly and strive for perfection in armed combat. Though they come from all walks of life, they are united by their singular commitment to engage in glorious battle. Many warriors serve as mercenary soldiers, while others become adventurers and danger-seeking fortune hunters. A typical warrior is strong, tough, and exceptionally violent.

### [Lingering Rage](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1323964) New talent

Fury tier 2 · Classic Era: Improved Demoralizing Shout

New Fury talent in the place of Classic's Improved Demoralizing Shout.

**Classic Era · Rank 5**

Increases the melee attack power reduction of your Demoralizing Shout by 40%.

**Forever · Rank 5**

Increases the time before your Rage begins to decay after leaving combat by 10 sec.

Details

- New in Forever's Fury tree (tier 2), in the slot Classic Era's Improved Demoralizing Shout had (Fury tier 2).

From the Forever client, compared with the Classic Era 1.15.9 client by spell ID (a rank with a new ID, by its Classic rank of the same name).
Classic's coefficients for ranks learned before level 20 include its low-level penalty; Forever gives those ranks their full value.
