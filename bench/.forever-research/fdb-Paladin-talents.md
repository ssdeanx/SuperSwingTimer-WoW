# Paladin talent calculator and leveling order

[Rotations & Guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin) · [PvE guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/pve) · [PvP guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/pvp) · [Talent builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/builds) · [Abilities](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/abilities) · [Talent Calculator](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/talents) · [Macros](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/macros) · [Changes vs Classic](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/changes)

Other classes: [Warrior](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/talents), [Hunter](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/talents), [Rogue](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/talents), [Priest](https://foreverdb.net/class/Priest/talents), [Shaman](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/talents), [Mage](https://foreverdb.net/class/Mage/talents), [Warlock](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warlock/talents), [Druid](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/talents)

Paladins are plate hybrids that heal, tank and fight in melee with Seals and Judgement. Forever overhauls them heavily: Holy Shock is a level 30 Holy talent and the new Light's Vigil (a level 40 talent) turns it into group healing, every Paladin gains Holy Strike at level 6, Protection gets Seal of Fury, Judgement of Fury, Swift Judgement and Templar's Bulwark, and Retribution gains Sacred Arbiter and the level 40 Unsealed Wrath talent, which rewards swapping seals mid-fight. Consecration and Blessing of Kings are trainer abilities at level 20.

Plan your Paladin talents for WoW Forever: click talents into the three trees and the calculator records the order you take them in, one talent per level, so you know what to pick at every level-up. Drag a level to move it, or click it to change its pick; every level is checked against the tree's tier and prerequisite rules. Share the build as a short link, load a recommended build in one click, and send the plan to the ForeverDB addon, which tells you where each new point goes in game.

## Talent points in WoW Forever

From the Forever client (TraitCurrencySource): one talent point per level from 10 to 60, 51 in all. The Legacy perk Talented gives the same points up to 5 levels sooner (one more rank each at levels 9, 8, 7, 6 and 5). In each section a row needs 5 more points spent in the rows above it.

## Paladin talents compared with Classic

Of the 51 talents in Forever's Paladin tree, 20 are new, 18 changed from Classic Era and 13 are the same. Classic talents Forever doesn't have: Improved Devotion Aura (Protection tier 1), Improved Concentration Aura (Protection tier 4), Blessing of Sanctuary (Protection tier 5), Improved Blessing of Might (Retribution tier 1), Improved Seal of the Crusader (Retribution tier 2), Improved Retribution Aura (Retribution tier 4), Sanctity Aura (Retribution tier 5), Consecration (Holy tier 3), Blessing of Kings (Protection tier 3).

## Holy talents

-   Tier 1: [Spiritual Focus](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20205) (5 ranks, level 10+): Gives your Flash of Light, Holy Light, and Light's Vigil spells a 70% chance to not lose casting time when you take damage.
-   Tier 1: [Divine Strength](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20262) (5 ranks, level 10+): Increases your Strength by 10%.
-   Tier 2: [Healing Light](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20237) (3 ranks, level 15+): Increases the amount healed by your Holy Light, Flash of Light, and Holy Shock spells by 12%.
-   Tier 2: [Reverence](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310899) (new in Forever) (2 ranks, level 15+): Allows 30% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.
-   Tier 2: [Improved Seals](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20224) (5 ranks, level 15+): Increases the damage done by your Seals and Judgements by 15%.
-   Tier 2: [Purifying Power](https://foreverdb.net/spell/429144) (new in Forever) (2 ranks, level 15+): Reduces the mana cost of your Cleanse and Purify spells by 20% and reduces the cooldown of your Exorcism and Holy Wrath spells by 33%.
-   Tier 3: [Divine Favor](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20216) (1 rank, level 20+): When activated, gives your next Flash of Light, Holy Light, or Holy Shock spell a 100% critical effect chance.
-   Tier 3: [Illumination](https://foreverdb.net/spell/20210) (changed from Classic) (5 ranks, level 20+): After getting a critical effect from your Flash of Light, Holy Light, Light's Vigil, or Holy Shock heal spell you have a 100% chance to gain Mana equal to 50% of the base cost of the spell. Classic: After getting a critical effect from your Flash of Light, Holy Light, or Holy Shock heal spell, gives you a 100% chance to gain Mana equal to the base cost of the spell.
-   Tier 3: [Holy Conduit](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1237268) (new in Forever) (2 ranks, level 20+): Reduces the mana cost of your Consecration, Holy Wrath, Exorcism, and Hammer of Wrath spells by 40%.
-   Tier 4: [Divine Precision](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310904) (new in Forever) (2 ranks, level 25+): Improves your chance to hit with Holy spells by 8%, and reduces the duration of Silence effects on you by 20%.
-   Tier 4: [Infusion of Light](https://foreverdb.net/spell/426065) (new in Forever) (2 ranks, level 25+): Your Holy Shock and Flash of Light critical hits reduce the cast time of your next Holy Light cast within 15 sec by 1.0 sec.
-   Tier 4: [Oath of Light](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1328548) (new in Forever) (1 rank, level 25+): Swear an Oath to protect target ally for 5 min, increasing the healing of your Holy Light on them by up to 400, your Flash of Light by up to 115, and your Holy Shock by up to 230. Only one Oath of Light may be sworn by the Paladin at any one time. (Numbers at level 60; they grow with your level.)
-   Tier 5: [Consecrated Ground](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310905) (new in Forever) (2 ranks, level 30+): Gives your Holy spells 10% increased damage against the first 4 enemies that enter your Consecration.
-   Tier 5: [Holy Shock](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1311606) (changed from Classic) (1 rank, level 30+): Blasts the target with Holy energy, causing 129 to 139 Holy damage to an enemy, or 110 to 118 healing to an ally. Classic: Blasts the target with Holy energy, causing 204 to 220 Holy damage to an enemy, or 204 to 220 healing to an ally.
-   Tier 5: [Boundless Light](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1328550) (new in Forever) (2 ranks, level 30+): Increases the critical strike chance of your Holy Shock spell by 30% when cast on allies not affected by your Oath of Light.
-   Tier 6: [Holy Power](https://foreverdb.net/spell/5923) (changed from Classic) (5 ranks, level 35+): Increases the critical strike healing bonus of your Holy spells by 50%. Classic: Increases the critical effect chance of your Holy spells by 5%.
-   Tier 7: [Light's Vigil](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310911) (new in Forever) (1 rank, level 40+): Applies Light's Vigil to a friendly target for 30 sec. The caster's next Holy Shock on the target incurs no cooldown and heals the target and their party for 315 to 333. Only one Vigil per Paladin may be active in each party.
