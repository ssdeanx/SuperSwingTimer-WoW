[Rotations & Guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter) · [PvE guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/pve) · [PvP guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/pvp) · [Talent builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/builds) · [Abilities](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/abilities) · [Talent Calculator](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/talents) · [Macros](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/macros) · [Changes vs Classic](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/changes)

Other classes: [Warrior](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/talents), [Paladin](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/talents), [Rogue](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/talents), [Priest](https://foreverdb.net/class/Priest/talents), [Shaman](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/talents), [Mage](https://foreverdb.net/class/Mage/talents), [Warlock](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warlock/talents), [Druid](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/talents)

Hunters are ranged physical damage dealers with pets, traps and stings. Forever adds Summon Hawk (a Beast Mastery talent: a hawk dive-bomb sharing Arcane Shot's cooldown), melee attack power on Aspect of the Beast, the Marksmanship capstone Sniper Shot and a real melee Survival toolkit with Strider Kick, Lacerate and a weapon-based Counterattack. Aimed Shot is a trainer ability at level 20 and shares its cooldown with Multi-Shot.

Plan your Hunter talents for WoW Forever: click talents into the three trees and the calculator records the order you take them in, one talent per level, so you know what to pick at every level-up. Drag a level to move it, or click it to change its pick; every level is checked against the tree's tier and prerequisite rules. Share the build as a short link, load a recommended build in one click, and send the plan to the ForeverDB addon, which tells you where each new point goes in game.

## Talent points in WoW Forever

From the Forever client (TraitCurrencySource): one talent point per level from 10 to 60, 51 in all. The Legacy perk Talented gives the same points up to 5 levels sooner (one more rank each at levels 9, 8, 7, 6 and 5). In each section a row needs 5 more points spent in the rows above it.

## Hunter talents compared with Classic

Of the 50 talents in Forever's Hunter tree, 14 are new, 17 changed from Classic Era and 19 are the same. Classic talents Forever doesn't have: Thick Hide (Beast Mastery tier 2), Improved Hunter's Mark (Marksmanship tier 2), Improved Scorpid Sting (Marksmanship tier 5), Improved Feign Death (Survival tier 4), Improved Serpent Sting (Marksmanship tier 4), Aimed Shot (Marksmanship tier 3).

## Beast Mastery talents

-   Tier 1: [Deadly Aspects](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19552) (changed from Classic) (5 ranks, level 10+): While Aspect of the Hawk is active, Auto Shot has a 10% chance of increasing ranged attack speed by 30% for 12 sec. While Aspect of the Beast is active, all melee auto attacks have a 10% chance of increasing melee attack speed by 30% for 12 sec. Classic: While Aspect of the Hawk is active, all normal ranged attacks have a 5% chance of increasing ranged attack speed by 30% for 12 sec.
-   Tier 1: [Endurance Training](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19583) (5 ranks, level 10+): Increases the Health and Armor of your pets by 15%.
-   Tier 2: [Focused Fire](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1223755) (new in Forever) (2 ranks, level 15+): Increases all damage you and your pet deal by 2% while your pet is active.
-   Tier 2: [Improved Aspect of the Monkey](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19549) (changed from Classic) (3 ranks, level 15+): Increases the Dodge bonus of your Aspect of the Monkey by 6%. Additionally, your pet gains 50% of the effect of your Aspect of the Monkey ability. Classic: Increases the Dodge bonus of your Aspect of the Monkey by 5%.
-   Tier 2: [Improved Revive Pet](https://foreverdb.net/spell/24443) (2 ranks, level 15+): Revive Pet's casting time is reduced by 6 sec, mana cost is reduced by 40%, and increases the health your pet returns with by an additional 30%.
-   Tier 3: [Pathfinding](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19559) (2 ranks, level 20+): Increases the speed bonus of your Aspect of the Cheetah and Aspect of the Pack by 6%.
-   Tier 3: [Bestial Swiftness](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19596) (1 rank, level 20+): Increases the movement speed of your pets by 30%.
-   Tier 3: [Unleashed Fury](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19616) (changed from Classic) (5 ranks, level 20+): Increases the damage done by your pets and hawks by 15%. Classic: Increases the damage done by your pets by 20%.
-   Tier 4: [Improved Mend Pet](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19572) (changed from Classic) (2 ranks, level 25+): Gives your Mend Pet spell a 50% chance of cleansing 1 Curse, Disease, Magic, or Poison effect from your pet each time it heals and reduces the Mana cost by 20%. Classic: Gives the Mend Pet spell a 50% chance of cleansing 1 Curse, Disease, Magic or Poison effect from the pet each tick.
-   Tier 4: [Ferocity](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19598) (changed from Classic) (5 ranks, level 25+): Increases the critical strike chance of your pets and hawks by 10%. Classic: Increases the critical strike chance of your pets by 15%.
-   Tier 4: [Summon Hawk](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1293241) (new in Forever) (1 rank, level 25+): Command a hawk to dive-bomb your targeted enemy, dealing 32 (+5% of ranged attack power) Physical damage and continuing its assault for 18 sec. Only 2 hawks can be active at once. Summon Hawk shares its cooldown with Arcane Shot.
-   Tier 5: [Spirit Bond](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19578) (changed from Classic) (2 ranks, level 30+): While your pet is active, you and your pet will regenerate 1% of total health every 5 sec. Classic: While your pet is active, you and your pet will regenerate 2% of total health every 10 sec.
-   Tier 5: [Intimidation](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19577) (changed from Classic) (1 rank, level 30+): Command your pet to Stun the target for 3 sec on its next successful attack, which also gains 100% increased critical strike chance. Generates high threat. Classic: Command your pet to intimidate the target on the next successful melee attack, causing a high amount of threat and stunning the target for 3 sec.
-   Tier 5: [Bestial Discipline](https://foreverdb.net/spell/19590) (changed from Classic) (2 ranks, level 30+): Increases the Focus regeneration of your pets by 20% and allows 50% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting. Classic: Increases the Focus regeneration of your pets by 20%.
