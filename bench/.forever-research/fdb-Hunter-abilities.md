# Hunter Abilities

[Rotations & Guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter) · [PvE guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/pve) · [PvP guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/pvp) · [Talent builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/builds) · [Abilities](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/abilities) · [Talent Calculator](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/talents) · [Macros](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/macros) · [Changes vs Classic](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/changes)

Other classes: [Warrior](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/abilities), [Paladin](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/abilities), [Rogue](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/abilities), [Priest](https://foreverdb.net/class/Priest/abilities), [Shaman](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/abilities), [Mage](https://foreverdb.net/class/Mage/abilities), [Warlock](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warlock/abilities), [Druid](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/abilities)

Hunters are ranged physical damage dealers with pets, traps and stings. Forever adds Summon Hawk (a Beast Mastery talent: a hawk dive-bomb sharing Arcane Shot's cooldown), melee attack power on Aspect of the Beast, the Marksmanship capstone Sniper Shot and a real melee Survival toolkit with Strider Kick, Lacerate and a weapon-based Counterattack. Aimed Shot is a trainer ability at level 20 and shares its cooldown with Multi-Shot.

## Beast Mastery (Ranged DPS)

Beast Mastery pushes damage through the pet and Forever's hawks. Unleashed Fury and Ferocity buff your pet and the hawks from Summon Hawk, a tier 4 Beast Mastery talent that shares Arcane Shot's cooldown, and the new Focused Fire adds 2% to everything you and your pet deal. Deadly Aspects gives Aspect of the Hawk a ranged haste proc, and Bestial Wrath is the burst window.

### Marksmanship (Ranged DPS)

Marksmanship is the classic Auto Shot weaving spec. In Forever, Aimed Shot is a trainer ability that shares its 6 sec cooldown with Multi-Shot, Careful Aim turns Intellect into Attack Power and opens Mortal Shots, and Trueshot Aura is a tier 4 talent from level 25. Rapid Killing and Rapid Recuperation turn kills and Serpent Sting into damage and mana, and Sniper Shot, a 4 sec cast with a 15 sec cooldown, is the level 40 capstone.

### Survival (Melee DPS)

Forever turns Survival into a viable melee hunter: Savage Strikes and Predator's Edge add melee crit and crit damage, Deflection adds up to 5% parry and Counterattack fires after parries. Strider Kick (a tier 5 talent) and Lacerate add a melee rotation, Aspect of the Beast gives melee attack power, and Expose Prey and Lacerating Strikes turn Mongoose Bite into a regular attack. You still Auto Shot and trap between melee bursts.

Best in slot gear at any level: [Beast Mastery](https://foreverdb.net/bis/hunter/beast-mastery), [Marksmanship](https://foreverdb.net/bis/hunter/marksmanship), [Survival](https://foreverdb.net/bis/hunter/survival)
