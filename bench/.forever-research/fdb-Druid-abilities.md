# Druid Abilities

[Rotations & Guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid) · [PvE guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/pve) · [PvP guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/pvp) · [Talent builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/builds) · [Abilities](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/abilities) · [Talent Calculator](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/talents) · [Macros](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/macros) · [Changes vs Classic](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/changes)

Other classes: [Warrior](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/abilities), [Paladin](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/abilities), [Hunter](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/abilities), [Rogue](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/abilities), [Priest](https://foreverdb.net/class/Priest/abilities), [Shaman](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/abilities), [Mage](https://foreverdb.net/class/Mage/abilities), [Warlock](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warlock/abilities)

Druids are shapeshifting hybrids that can heal, tank in bear form, fight in cat form or cast as Moonkin. Forever adds a real bear toolkit, and the new talent tree adds Lacerate, Feral Charge and Primal Bite for Feral and Wild Growth for Restoration, plus the out-of-combat Revive. Insect Swarm is now a Balance talent, and Omen of Clarity a trainer ability at 20.

## Balance (Caster DPS)

Balance casts Moonfire, Insect Swarm, Starfire and Wrath from range, and from level 40 does it in Moonkin Form, the Balance capstone. Insect Swarm is now a Balance talent (tier 4, level 25), Nature's Grace speeds your casting by 10% for 3 sec after non-periodic crits, and Omen of Clarity, now a passive every Druid learns from the trainer at 20, procs free spells.

### Feral Combat (Tank)

Feral Combat covers bear tanking and cat DPS. Forever gives bears a proper toolkit: Lacerate, a tier 4 talent, stacks a high-threat bleed up to 5 times, Feral Charge, a tier 3 talent from level 20, closes gaps and interrupts, and Primal Bite (Blizzard's renamed Mangle), the level 40 capstone, is a 6 sec cooldown bite with high threat. Maul remains the rage dump and Swipe the AoE threat.

### Restoration (Healer)

Restoration is a HoT healer that now has real group healing via Wild Growth, the tier 7 capstone: a party-wide front-loaded HoT on a 6 sec cooldown. Rejuvenation and Regrowth cover the tank, Swiftmend (now a tier 4 talent) instantly heals a target that has Rejuvenation or Regrowth, and Nature's Swiftness enables an instant Healing Touch.

Best in slot gear at any level: [Balance](https://foreverdb.net/bis/druid/balance), [Feral Combat](https://foreverdb.net/bis/druid/feral), [Restoration](https://foreverdb.net/bis/druid/restoration)
