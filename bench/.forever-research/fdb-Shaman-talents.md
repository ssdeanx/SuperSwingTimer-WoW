[Rotations & Guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman) · [PvE guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/pve) · [PvP guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/pvp) · [Talent builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/builds) · [Abilities](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/abilities) · [Talent Calculator](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/talents) · [Macros](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/macros) · [Changes vs Classic](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/changes)

Other classes: [Warrior](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/talents), [Paladin](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/talents), [Hunter](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/talents), [Rogue](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/talents), [Priest](https://foreverdb.net/class/Priest/talents), [Mage](https://foreverdb.net/class/Mage/talents), [Warlock](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warlock/talents), [Druid](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/talents)

Shamans are totem-based hybrids that heal, cast elemental spells or fight in melee. Forever adds Lightning Mastery as the Elemental capstone talent and Riptide as the Restoration capstone talent, Mental Dexterity and Rage of the Farseer for Enhancement, and quality-of-life totem tools: Call of the Elements, Call of the Ancestors and Call of the Spirits drop up to four totems in one 3 sec cast, and Totemic Recall returns them for mana.

Plan your Shaman talents for WoW Forever: click talents into the three trees and the calculator records the order you take them in, one talent per level, so you know what to pick at every level-up. Drag a level to move it, or click it to change its pick; every level is checked against the tree's tier and prerequisite rules. Share the build as a short link, load a recommended build in one click, and send the plan to the ForeverDB addon, which tells you where each new point goes in game.

## Talent points in WoW Forever

From the Forever client (TraitCurrencySource): one talent point per level from 10 to 60, 51 in all. The Legacy perk Talented gives the same points up to 5 levels sooner (one more rank each at levels 9, 8, 7, 6 and 5). In each section a row needs 5 more points spent in the rows above it.

## Shaman talents compared with Classic

Of the 50 talents in Forever's Shaman tree, 11 are new, 23 changed from Classic Era and 16 are the same. Classic talents Forever doesn't have: Shield Specialization (Enhancement tier 1), Enhancing Totems (Enhancement tier 3), Nature's Guidance (Restoration tier 3).

## Elemental Combat talents

-   Tier 1: [Convection](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16039) (5 ranks, level 10+): Reduces the mana cost of your Shock, Lightning Bolt, and Chain Lightning spells by 10%.
-   Tier 1: [Concussion](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16035) (5 ranks, level 10+): Increases the damage done by your Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, and Earth Shock spells by 5%.
-   Tier 2: [Elemental Devastation](https://foreverdb.net/spell/30160) (3 ranks, level 15+): Your Critical Strikes with direct damage spells increase your chance to get a Critical Strike with melee attacks by 9% for 10 sec.
-   Tier 2: [Reverberation](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16040) (changed from Classic) (5 ranks, level 15+): Reduces the cooldown of your Shock spells by 1.0 sec. Classic: Reduces the cooldown of your Shock spells by 1 sec.
-   Tier 2: [Elemental Alacrity](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16578) (changed from Classic) (3 ranks, level 15+): Reduces the cast time of your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells by 0.50 sec. Classic: Reduces the cast time of your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells by 1 sec.
-   Tier 2: [Call of Flame](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16038) (changed from Classic) (3 ranks, level 15+): Increases the damage done by your Fire Totems and by your Flame Shock and Fire Nova spells by 15%, as well as increases the duration of your Flame Shock spell by 9 sec. Classic: Increases the damage done by your Fire Totems by 15%.
-   Tier 3: [Elemental Focus](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16164) (1 rank, level 20+): Gives you a 10% chance to enter a Clearcasting state after casting any Fire, Frost, or Nature damage spell. The Clearcasting state reduces the mana cost of your next damage spell by 100%.
-   Tier 3: [Elemental Warding](https://foreverdb.net/spell/28996) (changed from Classic) (3 ranks, level 20+): Reduces damage taken from Fire, Frost, and Nature effects by 10%. Classic: Reduces damage taken from Fire, Frost and Nature effects by 10%.
-   Tier 4: [Trial by Fire](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16086) (changed from Classic) (2 ranks, level 25+): Increases the damage done by your Fire Nova spell by 20% and reduces its cooldown by 4 sec, and increases the duration of your Magma Totem by 20 sec. Classic: Reduces the delay before your Fire Nova Totem activates by 2 sec. and decreases the threat generated by your Magma Totem by 50%.
-   Tier 4: [Eye of the Storm](https://foreverdb.net/spell/29062) (changed from Classic) (3 ranks, level 25+): Gives your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells a 70% chance to not lose casting time when you take damage. Classic: Gives you a 100% chance to gain the Focused Casting effect that lasts for 6 sec after being the victim of a melee or ranged critical strike. The Focused Casting effect prevents you from losing casting time when taking damage.
-   Tier 4: [Earthbound](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1222988) (new in Forever) (1 rank, level 25+): Your Earthbind Totem Immobilizes nearby targets for 5 sec when cast.
-   Tier 5: [Elemental Reach](https://foreverdb.net/spell/28999) (2 ranks, level 30+): Increases the range of your Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, and Fire Nova spells by 6 yards.
-   Tier 5: [Call of Thunder](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16120) (changed from Classic) (1 rank, level 30+): Increases the critical strike chance of your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells by 5%. Classic: Increases the critical strike chance of your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells by an additional 6%.
-   Tier 5: [Lightning Overload](https://foreverdb.net/spell/408438) (new in Forever) (3 ranks, level 30+): Gives your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells a 30% chance to cast a second, similar spell on the same target at no additional cost that causes half damage and no threat.
-   Tier 6: [Elemental Fury](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16089) (changed from Classic) (5 ranks, level 35+): Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Searing and Magma Totems and your Fire, Frost, and Nature spells by 100%. Classic: Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Searing, Magma, and Fire Nova Totems and your Fire, Frost, and Nature spells by 100%.
-   Tier 7: [Lightning Mastery](https://foreverdb.net/spell/16166) (changed from Classic) (1 rank, level 40+): Gain mastery over Lightning, reducing the cast time of your Lightning Bolt spell by 0.5 sec, and removing the cooldown from your Chain Lightning spell. Lasts 15 sec. Classic: When activated, this spell gives your next Fire, Frost, or Nature damage spell a 100% critical strike chance and reduces the mana cost by 100%.
