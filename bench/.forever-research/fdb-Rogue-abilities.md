# Rogue Abilities

[Rotations & Guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue) · [PvE guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/pve) · [PvP guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/pvp) · [Talent builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/builds) · [Abilities](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/abilities) · [Talent Calculator](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/talents) · [Macros](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/macros) · [Changes vs Classic](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/changes) · [Poisons](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/poisons)

Rogues are energy-based melee assassins built around combo points, finishers and stealth openers. Forever gives them Shank (an off-hand strike from the trainer at 24), Mutilate (the Assassination capstone at level 40) and Venom (an Assassination talent that makes Shank boost your poisons), the Combat talents Coup de Grace for harder Eviscerates, Puncturing Wounds for Backstab crits and Flawless Execution, which makes Eviscerate cheaper, and Death Mark, Subtlety's capstone finisher.

## Assassination (Melee DPS)

Assassination is the poison spec in Forever: Mutilate, the level 40 capstone, hits with both weapons for 2 combo points and 20% extra damage against poisoned targets, and Venom makes each Shank raise your poison damage by 30% and your chance to apply poisons by 20% for 6 sec. Seal Fate and Cold Blood feed crits, and Eviscerate is the finisher.

### Combat (Melee DPS)

Combat is the steady sustained-damage spec using Sinister Strike or Backstab, Slice and Dice and Eviscerate with swords, axes, maces, fists or daggers. Hack and Slash is a single talent whose bonus follows your weapon (extra attacks for axes and swords, crit for daggers and fists, armor penetration for maces), Adrenaline Rush and Blade Flurry are the burst windows, and Flawless Execution, a tier 4 Combat talent, makes Eviscerate cost 15 less Energy. Dagger Combat builds with Backstab and Puncturing Wounds, a tier 2 Combat talent.

### Subtlety (Melee DPS)

Subtlety is an opener and burst spec with Premeditation, Ambush and Preparation, mostly for PvP and short fights. Hemorrhage is the builder and now increases your Rupture damage by 15%, and Serrated Blades adds armor penetration and 30% Rupture damage, making Rupture the main finisher. Death Mark, the level 40 capstone, is a finisher that stores part of your damage and deals it 6 sec later.

Best in slot gear at any level: [Assassination](https://foreverdb.net/bis/rogue/assassination), [Combat](https://foreverdb.net/bis/rogue/combat), [Subtlety](https://foreverdb.net/bis/rogue/subtlety)

## Common questions

### What's the best race for a Rogue in WoW Forever?

It depends on your faction and spec. ForeverDB's [best race for each class](https://foreverdb.net/launch/which-race) lists the picks, and every racial is on the [races page](https://foreverdb.net/races). Forever reworked most racials, so Classic advice does not carry over.

**Alliance:**
\- **Human**: Ruthless adds 1% critical strike chance to all spells and attacks, Will to Survive instantly removes all stuns, and Perception sharply raises stealth detection for 20 sec.
\- **Night Elf**: Quickness adds 1% dodge and 2% movement speed and makes you harder to detect in Stealth, Elune's Light adds 10% critical strike chance for 15 sec, and Shadowmeld drops you out of sight.
\- **Gnome**: Eureka! makes your non-periodic damaging abilities cost 10% less Energy and deal 10% more damage for 8 sec, and Escape Artist frees you from snares and roots.

**Horde:**
\- **Orc**: Blood Fury raises Attack Power and Spell Power by 10% for 15 sec, and Hardiness shortens stuns by 20%.
\- **Undead**: Will of the Forsaken removes Charm, Fear and Sleep, and Touch of the Grave gives your hits a 20% chance to drain Health (up to 5% of your maximum).
\- **Troll**: Berserking raises attack and casting speed by 10%, and Rapid Regeneration restores half your Health over 6 sec out of combat.

No race is uniquely best for a Rogue; pick for your spec and whether you PvP.

### How much damage does Mutilate do and when do Rogues get it?

Yes, but at level 40 now, not 30. In the current build Mutilate sits at tier 7 of the Assassination tree and needs **Venom at 1/1** first. The cap is 60, so you can reach it.

Rank 1, from the client: instantly attacks with both weapons for 90% weapon damage plus 40 with each weapon, with the damage raised by 20% against poisoned targets, and it awards 2 combo points. It costs 60 Energy. Higher ranks add more flat damage (Rank 2 plus 57, Rank 3 plus 79).

Mutilate needs a dagger in each hand even though the tooltip doesn't say so, and there's no positional requirement, so you don't have to stand behind the target. Everything Forever reworked for Rogues is on the [Rogue changes](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/changes) page.
