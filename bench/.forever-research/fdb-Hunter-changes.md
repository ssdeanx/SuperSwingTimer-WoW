Each spell lists every rank's damage or healing, spell power coefficient, cost, cast time, range and duration in both clients, written as "Forever instead of Classic". Classic Era is only the baseline: every Forever number comes from the Forever client's own data. Ranks learned above the level cap (60) are in the client but can't be reached yet.

## New Hunter talents

### [Improved Tracking](https://foreverdb.net/spell/24293) New talent (Classic Era: Monster Slaying)

New Survival talent in the place of Classic's Humanoid Slaying; moved to tier 1; renamed from Monster Slaying; 5 ranks instead of 3.

-   Renamed: Monster Slaying in Classic Era.
-   Moved: Survival tier 1, column 1 in Classic Era; Survival tier 1, column 2 in Forever.
-   5 ranks in Forever; Classic Era had 3.

**Classic Era (Rank 5):** Increases all damage caused against Humanoid targets by 3% and increases critical damage caused against Humanoid targets by an additional 3%.

**Forever (Rank 5):** While tracking Beasts, Demons, Dragonkin, Elementals, Giants, Humanoids, or Undead, all damage you deal to the tracked creature type is increased by 5%.

### [Careful Aim](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1223984) New talent

New Marksmanship talent, tier 2, 5 ranks, from level 15.

**Forever (Rank 5):** Increases your Attack Power by 100% of your Intellect.

### [Focused Fire](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1223755) New talent (Classic Era: Improved Eyes of the Beast)

New Beast Mastery talent in the place of Classic's Improved Eyes of the Beast.

**Classic Era (Rank 2):** Increases the duration of your Eyes of the Beast by 60 sec.

**Forever (Rank 2):** Increases all damage you and your pet deal by 2% while your pet is active.

### [Improved Stings](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310661) New talent

New Marksmanship talent, tier 2, 3 ranks, from level 15.

**Forever (Rank 3):** Increases the damage of your Serpent Sting ability by 20%, reduces the cooldown of your Viper Sting ability by 6 sec, and increases the duration of your Scorpid Sting ability by 45 sec.

### [Lone Wolf](https://foreverdb.net/spell/415370) New talent

New Marksmanship talent, tier 3, 1 rank, from level 20.

**Forever (Rank 1):** You deal 20% increased damage with all attacks while you do not have an active pet.

### [Rapid Killing](https://foreverdb.net/spell/415405) New talent

New Marksmanship talent, tier 3, 2 ranks, from level 20.

**Forever (Rank 2):** Reduces the cooldown on your Rapid Fire ability by 2 min. In addition, when you kill a non-trivial enemy or it dies while afflicted by your Serpent Sting, you gain Rapid Killing, increasing the damage of your next Shot ability within 20 sec by 20%.

### [Predator's Edge](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310627) New talent

New Survival talent, tier 4, 5 ranks, from level 25.

**Forever (Rank 5):** Increases your melee ability critical strike damage by 30% and your Off Hand weapon damage by 50%.

### [Summon Hawk](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1293241) New talent

New Beast Mastery talent, tier 4, 1 rank, from level 25.

**Forever (Rank 1):** Command a hawk to dive-bomb your targeted enemy, dealing 32 (+5% of ranged attack power) Physical damage and continuing its assault for 18 sec. Only 2 hawks can be active at once. Summon Hawk shares its cooldown with Arcane Shot.

### [Expose Prey](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310532) New talent (Classic Era: Killer Instinct)

New Survival talent in the place of Classic's Killer Instinct.

**Classic Era (Rank 2):** Increases your critical strike chance with all attacks by 3%.

**Forever (Rank 2):** Grants your melee and ranged attacks against enemies afflicted by Hunter's Mark a 10% chance to allow an activation of Mongoose Bite. Lasts 10 sec.

### [Rapid Recuperation](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1223987) New talent

New Marksmanship talent, tier 5, 2 ranks, from level 30.

**Forever (Rank 2):** Afflicting a target with Serpent Sting allows 50% of your Mana Regeneration to continue while casting for 15 sec, increased to 100% when Rapid Killing is consumed.

### [Resourcefulness](https://foreverdb.net/spell/440529) New talent

New Survival talent, tier 5, 2 ranks, from level 30.

**Forever (Rank 2):** Reduces the Mana cost of your Trap and melee abilities by 60%, and grants your melee and ranged ability Critical Strikes a 100% chance to allow 50% of your Mana Regeneration to continue while casting for 30 sec.

### [Strider Kick](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1317257) New talent

New Survival talent, tier 5, 1 rank, from level 30.

**Forever (Rank 1):** A powerful kick that deals 100% melee weapon damage and increases movement speed by 30% for 3 sec.

### [Survivalist's Discipline](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310496) New talent

New Survival talent, tier 5, 2 ranks, from level 30.

**Forever (Rank 2):** Reduces the cooldown of your Trap and Deterrence abilities by 40%.

### [Lacerating Strikes](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310533) New talent (Classic Era: Wyvern Sting)

New Survival talent in the place of Classic's Wyvern Sting.

**Classic Era (Rank 1):** A stinging shot that puts the target to sleep for 12 sec. Any damage will cancel the effect. When the target wakes up, the Sting causes 300 Nature damage over 6 sec. Only usable out of combat. Only one Sting per Hunter can be active on the target at a time.

**Forever (Rank 1):** Your Mongoose Bite also causes the target to Bleed for damage equal to 40% of the damage done by Mongoose Bite over 21 sec

### [Sniper Shot](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1310687) New talent

New Marksmanship talent, tier 7, 1 rank, from level 40.

**Forever (Rank 1):** A long-range shot that deals ranged damage plus 320 and increases the range of your next 3 Shots by 10 yards for 10 sec.
