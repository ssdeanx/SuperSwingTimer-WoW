# Paladin Abilities

[Rotations & Guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin) · [PvE guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/pve) · [PvP guide](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/pvp) · [Talent builds](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/builds) · [Abilities](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/abilities) · [Talent Calculator](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/talents) · [Macros](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/macros) · [Changes vs Classic](https://foreverdb.net/class/Paladin/changes)

Other classes: [Warrior](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warrior/abilities), [Hunter](https://foreverdb.net/class/Hunter/abilities), [Rogue](https://foreverdb.net/class/Rogue/abilities), [Priest](https://foreverdb.net/class/Priest/abilities), [Shaman](https://foreverdb.net/class/Shaman/abilities), [Mage](https://foreverdb.net/class/Mage/abilities), [Warlock](https://foreverdb.net/class/Warlock/abilities), [Druid](https://foreverdb.net/class/Druid/abilities)

Paladins are plate hybrids that heal, tank and fight in melee with Seals and Judgement. Forever overhauls them heavily: Holy Shock is a level 30 Holy talent and the new Light's Vigil (a level 40 talent) turns it into group healing, every Paladin gains Holy Strike at level 6, Protection gets Seal of Fury, Judgement of Fury, Swift Judgement and Templar's Bulwark, and Retribution gains Sacred Arbiter and the level 40 Unsealed Wrath talent, which rewards swapping seals mid-fight. Consecration and Blessing of Kings are trainer abilities at level 20.

## Holy (Healer)

Holy is a strong efficient single-target healer that now also has real group healing through Light's Vigil plus Holy Shock. Holy Light is the big heal and, at base values, also the most Mana-efficient one, Flash of Light is the fast cheap heal, and Illumination refunds mana on crits. Divine Favor guarantees a crit for emergencies.

### Protection (Tank)

Forever turns Protection into a real tank: Seal of Fury adds holy damage and, with a shield, absorb shields to every swing, while Judgement unleashes Judgement of Fury to damage and taunt. Holy Strike (from level 6) adds holy threat between Judgements, Consecration (trained at 20) handles packs, and Holy Shield rounds out threat. Mana comes back through Shield Specialization blocks (4% of your maximum Mana per block at 2/2, at most once every 3 sec). Swift Judgement adds a second taunt and Templar's Bulwark an emergency absorb shield.

### Retribution (Melee DPS)

Retribution wields a slow two-hander and relies on Seal of Command, crits and Vengeance. Forever gives every Paladin Holy Strike, an instant 10 sec cooldown attack, Sacred Arbiter to make it hit harder and refresh your Judgements, and Unsealed Wrath, the level 40 talent at the bottom of the section, which rewards swapping seals: your swings store copies of your active seal, and the first hit with a different seal gives each stored copy its normal chance to activate. Judgement and Exorcism fill the gaps.

Best in slot gear at any level: [Holy](https://foreverdb.net/bis/paladin/holy), [Protection](https://foreverdb.net/bis/paladin/protection), [Retribution](https://foreverdb.net/bis/paladin/retribution)

## Common questions

### What does the Paladin's Holy Strike do and when do you learn it?

Holy Strike is a melee strike every Paladin trains at level 6. It is instant, costs a little mana and has a 10 second cooldown, so it is your main damage and threat button while levelling.

It hits for a share of your weapon damage plus a set amount of Holy damage, and both grow with each rank. Rank 1 does 25% weapon damage plus 10 to 14 Holy; the linked [Rank 3](https://foreverdb.net/spell/1866) does 32% plus 17 to 21 (learned at level 20); the top Rank 8 reaches 50% weapon damage plus 81 to 105 Holy at level 60. There are eight ranks in all, trained from level 6 to 60.

Every spec has a talent built around it: Holy Power in Holy, Iron Creed in Protection and Sacred Arbiter in Retribution. **Iron Creed** (Protection, from level 35, three ranks) raises Holy Strike's threat by 10% per rank, 30% at 3/3, and while Righteous Fury is up it also makes Holy Strike cut your damage taken by 2% per rank, 6% at 3/3, for 6 sec. That is why it anchors Paladin tanking.
